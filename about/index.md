## The company

Zeth Ltd provides [contract engineering]({{ '/services/contract-engineering/' | relative_url }}) to technical teams and [bespoke application development]({{ '/services/app-development/' | relative_url }}) to organisations that want something built. It also supports [open-source software]({{ '/open-source/' | relative_url }}), including the Holder project.

We are not an agency, and we do not pretend to be one. Keeping the company small means direct communication, clear responsibility and software built by someone who expects to be accountable for it for years to come.
