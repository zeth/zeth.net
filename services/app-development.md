**Already have a web application or an in-house team?** We can build the app that works alongside it, or deliver a self-contained piece of work your developers don't have time for, and hand it over cleanly.

## How a project works

<ol class="steps">
  <li>
    <h3>Talk through the idea</h3>
    <p>We discuss what you want to achieve, who will use the app and what success looks like. There is no obligation at this stage.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Agree a practical scope</h3>
    <p>Together we decide what the first version needs to do, which devices it should run on, and a realistic budget and timeline.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Prototype the important choices</h3>
    <p>Where it helps, we build a quick prototype of the key screens or trickiest feature so you can see and try it before full development starts.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Build the application</h3>
    <p>The app is built in stages, with regular check-ins so you can see progress and give feedback along the way.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Test it properly</h3>
    <p>We test across the phones, tablets or computers your users actually have, and fix what we find before anyone else does.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Release and look after it</h3>
    <p>We prepare the app for distribution, for example through the app stores, and agree how updates and ongoing maintenance will work.</p>
  </li>
</ol>
