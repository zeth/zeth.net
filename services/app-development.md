We build mobile and desktop applications, backend services and integrations for businesses, charities and communities. That can mean a new app, a mobile companion to your Django application, or a defined project delivered into your existing codebase.

We agree the scope, budget, timeline and handover with you, including any ongoing maintenance. You can work with us whether you have an in-house development team or are commissioning software for the first time.

If you want an engineer working within your team's day-to-day priorities and process, see [Join your team]({{ '/services/contract-engineering/' | relative_url }}).

## How a project works

<ol class="steps">
  <li>
    <h3>Talk through the idea</h3>
    <p>We discuss what you want to achieve, who will use the software and what success looks like. There is no obligation at this stage.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Agree a practical scope</h3>
    <p>Together we decide what the first version needs to do, which devices or systems it needs to work with, and a realistic budget and timeline.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Prototype the important choices</h3>
    <p>Where it helps, we build a quick prototype of the key screens or trickiest feature so you can see and try it before full development starts.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Build the software</h3>
    <p>The software is built in stages, with regular check-ins so you can see progress and give feedback along the way.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Test it properly</h3>
    <p>We test the agreed features on the devices and systems your users will use, address the issues we find and review readiness for release with you.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3>Release and look after it</h3>
    <p>We prepare the software for deployment or distribution, hand it over with the documentation you need, and agree how updates and ongoing maintenance will work.</p>
  </li>
</ol>
